DMCA & Copyright Policy

Last Updated: November 15, 2026

1. Respect for Copyright

Walloraa respects the intellectual property rights of artists, photographers, and creators worldwide. Wallpapers curated on Walloraa are intended to be original creations, AI-assisted digital renders, or CC0 / royalty-free visuals.

2. Filing a Copyright Takedown Notice

If you are a copyright owner (or authorized agent) and believe any image hosted on Walloraa infringes upon your copyright, please send a takedown notice containing:

  • Identification of the copyrighted work claimed to have been infringed.
  • The exact Walloraa URL(s) where the image appears (e.g., https://walloraa.vercel.app/wallpaper.html?id=...).
  • Your full name, organization (if applicable), and contact email address.
  • A statement in good faith that the disputed use is not authorized by the copyright owner.

3. Rapid Removal Commitment

Send DMCA notices to support@walloraa.com or via our Contact Page. Verified removal requests are processed and permanently deleted from our cloud storage within 24 to 48 hours.
