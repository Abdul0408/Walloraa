Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 15, 2026

1. Information We Collect

Walloraa (walloraa.vercel.app) is an open-source 4K & 8K wallpaper gallery. Visitors can browse and download wallpapers without creating an account or submitting personal information. We only collect standard non-personally identifiable server logs (such as browser type, device resolution, referring pages, and approximate region) to keep the site fast and reliable.

2. Cookies & Google AdSense

We use third-party advertising companies, including Google AdSense, to serve ads when you visit Walloraa. Google uses cookies (including the DoubleClick DART cookie) to serve ads to users based on their prior visits to Walloraa and other websites on the Internet.

Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or www.aboutads.info.

3. Third-Party Infrastructure

Walloraa uses trusted cloud infrastructure providers—including Vercel (hosting), Supabase (database), and Cloudflare R2 (content delivery)—to deliver high-resolution wallpapers globally. These providers process standard network requests in accordance with their respective privacy policies.

4. Children's Privacy

Walloraa does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided personal data through our contact channels, please contact us immediately for removal.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy, reach us via our Contact Page or email support@walloraa.com.
